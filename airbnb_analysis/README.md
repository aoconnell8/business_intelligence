# # Airbnb Analysis — Key Findings

1. 198 hosts across the three cities each run 10 or more listings, and in Chicago those hosts control 31.9% of all listings — nearly a third of the market belongs to a small group of multi-property operators.

2. In Columbus's Near East, Near North/University, Near South, and five other qualifying neighborhoods, North Linden has the lowest median price per person ($32.60) for family-sized entire homes with strong reviews, making it the most budget-friendly pick for a family of four.

3. Twin Cities entire homes earn a median estimated revenue of $19,619 per year, the lowest of the three cities' entire-home segments despite Twin Cities charging a comparable median nightly price to Chicago.

4. Columbus has the lowest share of "mostly taken" entire homes in October 2026 at 19.0%, meaning Columbus hosts have more open availability than Chicago (21.8%) or Twin Cities (20.0%).

5. Superhosts in Chicago charge a $43.80 median price premium over non-superhosts, while in Twin Cities the premium is actually slightly negative (-$1.38), showing the Superhost pricing advantage is not consistent across cities.
